DATA & METHOD

Designed to avoid fake “meta” certainty.

Live weapon and attachment data are pulled from the BF6 Weapon Analyzer project when available. A second rules layer models class weapon proficiencies, current class traits, training paths, gadget/throwable utility and complementary secondary selection, plus mechanics that raw numerical simulators can otherwise treat as “no effect,” such as Range Finder, Magwell Flare and DLC Bolt. Point costs are audited before a data sync is published; unknown costs are rejected rather than guessed.
