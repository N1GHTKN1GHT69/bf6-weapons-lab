How to read the numbers
TRIGGER→KILL ↓Lower is better. Trigger pull to lethal bullet impact at the selected distance; includes projectile flight.
MECH TTK ↓Lower is better. First damaging hit to lethal hit; excludes projectile flight. A one-shot kill is 0 ms.
BTK ↓Lower is better. Bullets or shells required to kill a 100 HP unarmored target.
DAMAGE ↑Higher is generally better, but breakpoints matter more than raw damage once BTK is unchanged.
LASER SCORE ↑Higher is better, 0–100. Filter-stable Laserbeam efficiency score. Ranking uses a fixed 55/45 percentage tradeoff between Trigger→Kill and Beam Index; changing class filters does not change that tradeoff.
BEAM INDEX ↓Lower is better. Absolute mechanical instability index built from recoil, recoil unpredictability and ADS spread at this distance.
ADS RECOIL ↓Lower is better. Mechanical recoil magnitude after the winning attachments when exhaustive build data is active.
RECOIL VARIATION ↓Lower is better. Directional variation in recoil; more variation means a less repeatable pattern.
UNPREDICTABLE RECOIL ↓Lower is better. Portion of recoil converted into unpredictable lateral movement by directional variation.
SUSTAINED ADS SPREAD ↓Lower is better. Effective spread while continuing to fire ADS after spread growth and recovery.
MOVING ADS SPREAD ↓Lower is better. Minimum ADS spread while moving/strafe-firing.
OPTIC FIT ↑Higher is better, 0–100. Range suitability of the selected optic tier; this is an optimizer policy, not claimed magnification data.
VELOCITY ↑Higher is generally better. Faster bullets reduce flight time and make moving targets easier to lead.
ADS TIME ↓Lower is better. Time to aim down sights.
ROFContextual. Higher fire rate can reduce TTK, but only together with damage, BTK, recoil and spread.
WEAPON CHARACTER BARS ↑Higher bar is better relative to other weapons in the same class; these are descriptive bars, not the META score.

DATA & AUDITData status

AUTO uses LASERBEAM META: a pool-stable percentage utility of 55% exact-distance trigger→kill lethality and 45% transformed recoil/spread controllability. Optic tiers are range-gated so a cheap close-range sight cannot win a long-range build simply by saving Pick points. Weapons more than 25% off the fastest kill pace receive an additional penalty. Mechanical TTK remains visible separately. Exhaustive builds use the Analyzer's attachment transforms, recoil amount, directional variation and effective ADS spread; missing audited data fails closed. Community tier lists, popularity and creator recommendations are never inputs.

Attachment display-name verification is a separate concern from optimization. Name confidence never changes attachment candidates, modifiers, point costs, budgets or ranking.
